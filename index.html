Seasonal Timeline Overview

Spring

Start indoor seeds 6 weeks prior to final frost. Transplant hardy cold-season crops outdoors.

Summer

Plant warm-season produce (Tomatoes, Peppers). Maintain consistent irrigation and mulch.

Autumn

Sow fall greens (Kale, Spinach, Garlic). Prepare beds with organic compost for winter.

Winter

Propagate indoor houseplants, service garden tools, and plan upcoming crop rotations.
