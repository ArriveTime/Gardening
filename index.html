GENERAL SEASONAL TIMELINE

  • SPRING: Start seeds indoor 6 weeks before last frost. Transplant hardy greens.
  • SUMMER: Plant heat-tolerant crops (Peppers, Tomatoes). Monitor daily watering.
  • AUTUMN: Sow cool-season vegetables (Kale, Spinach, Garlic). Mulch soil beds.
  • WINTER: Indoor microgreens, compost preparation, tool maintenance.
